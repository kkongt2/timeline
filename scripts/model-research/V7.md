# Outcome-aware history, race pace and prospective evaluation

## Scope and chronology

The general v5 and selective v6 models remain independently available. Version 7
compares corrected outcome/identity features with a pace-feature ablation. It
does not use live odds, current-race results or current-race sectionals as input.
Every history feature uses strictly earlier dates, including jockey/trainer
statistics and reference clocks. The entire date is frozen before its outcomes
are appended.

The configuration is selected on July–December 2025. Final fitting uses data
before January 2026, calibration uses January–February, and July 16 onward is a
reused historical audit. The audit overlaps earlier research and cannot be called
an independent test. The fixed production gate requires at least 300 paired races,
a positive lower endpoint of the 95% date-cluster bootstrap uplift interval, and
no increase in all-candidate Brier loss. Failing models remain comparison-only.

## Data and identity

All three KRA report layouts are parsed separately where necessary: Jeju weights
and clock notation differ, and Busan has seven running-position columns. Actual
place winners are read from the settled payout section. Top-three history remains
a separate feature. Known DNF/DQ losers can be represented only if the complete
field and official winners are unambiguous; unsupported and ambiguous results are
excluded and counted. No exclusion is silently changed into a losing label.

Entry-page permanent horse IDs are retained and checked when available. Historical
text reports do not contain those IDs, so matching also includes venue, name,
birth cohort inferred from the reported age, and sex. Renames/transfers and sex
classification changes may fragment history; uncertain identities are not merged.

## Automatic pre-race records

`scripts/snapshot.cjs` runs in the collector workflow after fetching cards. It
freezes the first successful observation 15–90 minutes before scheduled start,
with a source age no more than 30 minutes. GitHub scheduled jobs may be delayed;
missed windows are not backfilled. Records include the exact input, input and
model/code hashes, timestamp, and all probabilities for the served model and
available challenger. General and selective candidates are distinct tracks.

`data/predictions/YYYYMMDD.json` only gains previously unseen race IDs. Existing
snapshots are never replaced. Official outcomes live separately under
`data/settlements/`. Delayed outcomes can be recovered after race cards disappear
from the date selector. Missing starters/results are pending; withdrawn selections
and refunds are excluded. `prediction-summary.json` presents prospective counts
and compares models only on identical, settled, eligible races.

Recomputed historical UI candidates are not prospective forecasts. Actual future
performance starts accumulating only after this functionality is deployed.

## Validation

- `python tests/test_v7.py`: temporal isolation, payout-aware place features,
  cohort separation, serving/training parity and rival-dependent pair features.
- `node tests/model-v7.cjs`: serialized estimator and full race parity.
- `node tests/snapshot.cjs`: immutable forecasts, freshness/time gates, settlement,
  no historical backfill, refund and withdrawal exclusion.
- Existing v6 model and UI checks protect existing recommendations and navigation.
